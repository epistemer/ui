| # | Finding | Verdict | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ui's forms are `novalidate`, so `setCustomValidity` blocks no submit; the size check showed an error and the form went anyway | Valid | A capturing `submit` listener refuses the form and focuses the field; design §4.3 and §8.1 finding 9 |
